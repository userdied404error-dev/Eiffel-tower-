About the Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in the world, located in Paris, France. It was designed by Gustave Eiffel and completed in 1889 for the World's Fair.

Standing at 330 meters tall, the tower attracts millions of visitors every year and offers breathtaking views of Paris.

History

Construction of the Eiffel Tower began in 1887 and took about two years to complete. Initially criticized by artists and intellectuals, it later became one of the world's most admired structures.
